# Amazon E-commerce Business Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project analyses Amazon e-commerce sales and product performance using sales transaction data and Glance Views data.

The analysis covers the period from 1 May 2019 to 31 August 2019. It focuses on understanding product performance, revenue generation, sales trends, promotional activity, pricing and unit conversion.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Identify revenue-generating and non-revenue-generating SKUs.
- Analyse product-level sales and average selling price.
- Identify potential promotional event dates.
- Evaluate sales performance before and after the potential sale event.
- Identify slow-growing subcategories.
- Examine the relationship between average selling price and unit conversion.
- Identify data quality issues that may affect business decisions.

## 3. Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas and analytical functions
- Statistical analysis

## 4. Dataset Overview
The project uses two datasets:

- Sales Data: Contains sales transactions, revenue and ordered units.
- Glance Views Data: Contains product visibility and view-related information.

Analysis period: May 2019 – August 2019.

## 5. Key Business Findings
- 365 out of 465 SKUs generated revenue, representing approximately 78.5%.
- 48 SKUs had sales between May and July but recorded zero revenue in August.
- 15–16 July 2019 were identified as likely sale event dates based on unusually high revenue and ordered units.
- No evidence of a sustained post-event sales decline was found in the comparison period.
- Tablet Stands and Docks recorded a 96.88% revenue decline from May to August, while its parent category grew by 59.29%.
- No statistically significant relationship was found between average selling price and unit conversion in the analysed sample.

## 6. Data Quality Observations
The analysis identified:
- Missing SKU-date matches between the two datasets.
- Missing REP_OOS values.
- REP_OOS values above 100.

These issues were documented rather than silently corrected or imputed.

## 7. Business Recommendations
- Investigate SKUs with no revenue in August to check inventory and listing status.
- Review underperforming subcategories and identify possible product or demand-related issues.
- Monitor sales trends around promotional events.
- Evaluate pricing decisions using both revenue and conversion measures.
- Improve data consistency between sales and product visibility records.

## 8. Conclusion
The project demonstrates how e-commerce sales and product visibility data can be analysed to identify business trends, product performance issues and opportunities for improvement.

The findings provide a data-driven foundation for product monitoring, pricing evaluation and business decision-making.
